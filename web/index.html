What is TIVOX?

Most tokens are built to be exciting. TIVOX is built to be boring in the places where boring matters. It is a fixed-supply ERC-20 token with no hidden mint function, no surprise inflation, and no owner backdoor to print more. What you see is what exists: exactly 1,000,000 TVX, forever.

Holders can burn their own tokens, but no one can create more. The contract is open-source and verified on-chain for anyone to check. Everything else is just the ox, still pulling.

Principles

Fixed supply

There is no mint function in the contract. Exactly 1,000,000 TVX were created at deployment, and that number can only ever shrink through burning. Not even the owner can create more.

Transparent

The full source code is verified on Etherscan. No hidden logic, no upgradeable proxy, no private backdoor. Anyone can read exactly what the contract does.

No promises

TIVOX is not an investment product. There is no liquidity pool, no exchange listing, and no price. It exists as a small, honest piece of on-chain engineering.

FAQ

Can the supply ever increase?

No. There is no mint function, and the total supply is fixed at deployment. Holders may burn their own tokens, so the supply can only decrease.

Is it on mainnet?

No. TIVOX currently lives on the Sepolia testnet for development and demonstration. A mainnet deployment has not been planned.

Can I buy TVX?

No. There is no liquidity pool and no exchange listing. TVX is not tradeable. Anyone asking you to pay for it is a scam.

Who controls the contract?

Ownership is explicit and transferable on-chain. It grants no special powers: no minting, no pausing, no freezing, and no ability to take tokens from anyone.
