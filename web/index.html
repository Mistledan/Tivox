What is TIVOX?

Everything in this market runs on panic. TIVOX doesn't. The ox is slow, stubborn and impossible to move once it has decided where it's going — it doesn't chase the next shiny thing, it doesn't spook at a red candle, it just keeps pulling the plough. That's the whole idea, and the contract is built the same way.

TIVOX is a fixed-supply ERC-20 token — no hidden mint function, no surprise inflation, no owner backdoor to print more. What you see is what exists: exactly 1,000,000 TVX, forever. Holders can burn their own tokens, but no one can create more. The contract is fully open-source and verified on-chain for anyone to check.

Why an ox?

Because nothing else in this space moves like one. An ox is not fast and it is not clever. It has no opinion on what the chart did this morning. It was pointed at a direction, and it is still going that way — through mud, through noise, through whatever everyone else is panicking about this week.

Most tokens are built to be exciting. This one is built to be boring in the places where boring matters: the supply can't change, the owner can't print, and the code is sitting on Etherscan for anyone to read. Everything else is just the ox, still pulling.

How to get Sepolia ETH (it's free)

This is a Sepolia testnet token, so real money is never needed. To interact with it you need test ETH — grab some from a Sepolia faucet:

  • 1. Add the Sepolia network to MetaMask (or switch to it: Ethereum → Sepolia test network).
  • 2. Copy your wallet address from MetaMask.
  • 3. Get free Sepolia ETH from a faucet, e.g. sepoliafaucet.com or Chainlink Faucet.
  • 4. Paste your address, request, and the ETH arrives in under a minute.
  • 5. Come back, connect your wallet, and check your TVX balance.

TVX is not for sale, has no liquidity pool, and is not tradeable — it exists purely as an on-chain proof of the fixed-supply contract.

FAQ

Can the supply ever increase?

No. There is no mint function in the contract, verified on Etherscan. Exactly 1,000,000 TVX were created at deployment and that number can only shrink (holders may burn their own tokens).

Is it on mainnet?

No — currently on Sepolia testnet for development. Mainnet deployment has not been planned.

Can I buy TVX?

No. There is no liquidity pool and no exchange listing. If you receive TVX it is a gift for testing. Anyone asking you to pay is a scam.

Who can mint?

No one. The contract has no mint function at all, so not even the owner can create more TVX. The owner also cannot pause transfers, freeze wallets, or take tokens back.
