Principles

Three rules, never rewritten

These are enforced by the contract itself, not by a team, a roadmap, or a promise.

∞

Fixed supply

1,000,000,000 TVX minted once. There is no mint function, so the number can only decrease through voluntary burning.

✓

Locked liquidity

80% of the supply sits in the Uniswap pool and the LP tokens are burned to the dead address. It is a permanent lock, not a promise.

🔒

No privileged control

No mint, no pause, no freeze, no seizure. Ownership is explicit and transferable on-chain, and can be renounced at any time.

FAQ

Common questions

Can the supply ever increase?
No. There is no mint function, and the total supply is fixed at deployment. Holders may burn their own tokens, so the supply can only decrease.
Where does TVX trade?
On Uniswap V2 over Base. The liquidity is 80% of the total supply paired with ETH, and the LP tokens are burned, so the pool can never be drained by the team.
Is there a buy or sell tax?
No. TIVOX has 0% transfer tax. Sending, buying, and selling cost nothing extra — you keep every token you buy.
Is the liquidity locked?
Yes — permanently. The pool's LP tokens are sent to the dead address at launch, which makes them unspendable forever. Check the pair on BaseScan if you want to verify.
What is the community allocation?
20% of the supply is vested in a public time-lock contract: nothing can be released for the first 30 days, then it unlocks linearly over 6 months to a beneficiary wallet. That removes any risk of the community share dumping at launch, and every release is visible on-chain.
Who controls the contract?
No one has special powers: there is no minting, pausing, freezing, or seizure. Ownership is on-chain and can be renounced. The contract itself just cannot do those things.
