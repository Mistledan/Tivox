Overview

Built to be boring where it matters

Most tokens are designed to be exciting. TIVOX is designed to be predictable: simple rules, stated up front, and impossible to quietly change after deployment.

🐂

A fixed supply

Exactly 1,000,000 TVX were created at deployment. There is no mint function anywhere in the contract, so no address — including the owner — can ever create more.

🔍

Open and verified

The full source code is verified on Etherscan. No hidden logic, no upgradeable proxy, no private backdoor. Anyone can read exactly what the contract does.

🛡️

No promises

TIVOX is not an investment product. There is no liquidity pool, no exchange listing, and no price. It exists as a small, honest piece of on-chain engineering.

Principles

Three rules, never rewritten

These are enforced by the contract itself, not by a team, a roadmap, or a promise.

∞

Fixed supply

No mint function. Exactly 1,000,000 TVX exist, and the number can only decrease through voluntary burning.

✓

Transparent

The complete source is verified on Etherscan. There is no upgrade mechanism and no admin function that can change behavior later.

🔒

No privileged control

Ownership grants no ability to mint, pause, freeze, or seize tokens. It exists for transparency and handover only.

FAQ

Common questions

Can the supply ever increase?
No. There is no mint function, and the total supply is fixed at deployment. Holders may burn their own tokens, so the supply can only decrease.
Is it on mainnet?
No. TIVOX currently lives on the Sepolia testnet for development and demonstration. A mainnet deployment has not been planned.
Can I buy TVX?
No. There is no liquidity pool and no exchange listing. TVX is not tradeable, and anyone asking you to pay for it is a scam.
Who controls the contract?
Ownership is explicit and transferable on-chain, but it grants no special powers: no minting, no pausing, no freezing, and no ability to take tokens from anyone.
Where can I read the full details?
The whitepaper documents the token specification, contract architecture, security model, transparency, risks, and roadmap.
