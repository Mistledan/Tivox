Abstract

TIVOX is a fixed-supply ERC-20 token deployed on the Sepolia testnet. It was created to demonstrate a simple idea: a token whose monetary policy cannot be changed after deployment. There is no mint function, no upgrade mechanism, and no privileged control that can alter supply or move user funds. Exactly 1,000,000 TVX exist, and that number can only ever decrease through voluntary burning.

TIVOX does not claim to be an investment, a currency, or a store of value. It is a piece of verifiable on-chain engineering, published openly for anyone to inspect.

1. Introduction

Most token projects are designed around growth narratives: emissions, incentives, treasury control, and the ability to adjust rules later. TIVOX deliberately removes all of that. What remains is a contract that does one thing and does it without ambiguity.

The project's mascot, the ox, reflects that stance. The ox is not fast and it is not clever. It does not react to market noise. It was pointed in a direction and it keeps going. The token is built the same way: simple rules, stated up front, and impossible to quietly change.

3. Contract architecture

3.1 Fixed supply

The entire supply is minted once in the constructor and assigned to the deploying address. Because the contract contains no mint function, no address — including the owner — can ever create additional tokens. The monetary policy is therefore immutable by construction, not by policy.

3.2 Ownership

TIVOX inherits OpenZeppelin's Ownable so that ownership is explicit and transferable on-chain rather than held by an anonymous deployer. Ownership grants no special powers over the token: it cannot mint, pause transfers, freeze balances, or seize tokens. It exists purely for transparency and future handover. The owner can transfer or renounce ownership at any time.

3.3 Burning

Holders can burn their own tokens, permanently removing them from circulation. Because supply can only decrease and never increase, burning is the only supply-side action available to anyone.

3.4 No upgradeability

The contract is not a proxy and cannot be upgraded. Once deployed, its logic is final. There is no admin function that can change behavior after the fact.

4. Transparency and verification

The full source code is published and verified on Etherscan, meaning the deployed bytecode is matched against readable source that anyone can review. The contract address and verification link are listed on the landing page and in the footer of this document.

All on-chain activity — transfers, burns, and ownership changes — is publicly visible on Sepolia Etherscan.

6. Network and status

TIVOX is live on the Sepolia testnet. Testnet tokens have no monetary value and exist only for development and demonstration. As a result, there is intentionally no liquidity, no market, and no way to exchange TVX for anything.

9. FAQ

Can the supply ever increase?

No. There is no mint function, and the total supply is fixed in the constructor.

Who can mint?

No one. The contract has no mint function at all.

Can the owner take my tokens?

No. Ownership grants no ability to move, freeze, or seize tokens.

Is TVX for sale?

No. It is not listed anywhere and has no liquidity. It cannot be bought or sold.

Will there be a mainnet version?

There is no mainnet plan at this time.
