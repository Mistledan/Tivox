Abstract

TIVOX is a tradable ERC-20 token deployed on the Base network. It has a total supply of 1,000,000,000 TVX, a 0% transfer tax, and no mint function — the supply can never increase and can only decrease through voluntary burning.

80% of the supply is paired with ETH in the Uniswap V2 pool at launch, and the pool's LP tokens are sent to the dead address, permanently locking that liquidity. The remaining 20% goes to a single, publicly-labeled community wallet. There is no privileged control: ownership exists on-chain but grants no ability to mint, pause, freeze, or seize tokens, and it can be renounced at any time.

TIVOX is not an investment vehicle and makes no price or return promises. It is a straightforward, transparent, community-owned token built on simple rules that cannot be changed later.

1.Introduction

Most token projects are designed around growth narratives: emissions, incentives, treasury control, and the ability to adjust rules later. TIVOX deliberately removes all of that. What remains is a contract that does one thing and does it without ambiguity.

The project's mascot, the ox, reflects that stance. The ox is not fast and it is not clever. It does not react to market noise. It was pointed in a direction and it keeps going. The token is built the same way: simple rules, stated up front, and impossible to quietly change.

This document is the definitive specification for the mainnet token. The earlier Sepolia testnet deployment remains only as a historical engineering artifact and is unrelated to the market.

3.Contract architecture

3.1 Fixed supply

The entire supply is minted once in the constructor and assigned to the deploying address. Because the contract contains no mint function, no address — including the owner — can ever create additional tokens. The monetary policy is therefore immutable by construction, not by policy.

3.2 Zero tax

The contract is a plain ERC-20 with no fee hooks, no reflections, and no hidden deductions. Every transfer moves the exact amount requested. This keeps trading fair and cheap for everyone.

3.3 Burning

Holders can burn their own tokens, permanently removing them from circulation. Because the supply can only decrease and never increase, burning is the only supply-side action available to anyone.

3.4 Ownership without power

TIVOX inherits OpenZeppelin's Ownable only so that ownership is explicit and transferable on-chain. Ownership grants no special powers: it cannot mint, pause, freeze, or seize tokens. Ownership can be transferred or renounced at any time, and renouncing it is part of the launch procedure.

3.5 No upgradeability

The contract is not a proxy and cannot be upgraded. Once deployed, its logic is final. There is no admin function that can change behavior after the fact.

4.Distribution & liquidity

The full 1,000,000,000 TVX is minted to the deployer at construction, then distributed in one transparent step at launch:

Because the LP tokens are burned at creation, the initial liquidity is permanently locked. No mechanism exists to withdraw it. The community wallet is publicly labeled and every one of its transactions is visible on-chain; it holds no privileged access to the contract.

5.Transparency & verification

The full source code is published and verified on BaseScan, meaning the deployed bytecode is matched against readable source that anyone can review. The contract address, pair address, and community wallet appear on this page and across the project's channels.

All on-chain activity — transfers, burns, and ownership changes — is publicly visible. Anyone can independently verify the claimed mechanics rather than trusting this document.

9.FAQ

Can the supply ever increase?
No. There is no mint function, and the total supply is fixed at deployment.
Is there a tax?
No. TIVOX has a 0% transfer tax. Nothing is deducted from trades.
Is liquidity locked?
Yes. The LP tokens are burned to the dead address at launch, which makes them permanently unspendable.
Where can I buy TVX?
On Uniswap V2 over the Base network. Always verify the contract address first.
Can the owner take my tokens?
No. Ownership grants no ability to move, freeze, or seize tokens, and it is being renounced at launch.
